Writing to us

Useful to include, if you have it.

None of this is required. A single paragraph describing the frustration is a perfectly good first message.

  • The problem, not the technology

    What takes too long, costs too much or goes wrong too often. The technology choice is our job, not yours.

  • Who is affected

    Which team does the work today, roughly how many people, and how technical they are.

  • Where the data lives

    The systems involved, and whether they are on Microsoft, another cloud or on-premises. An approximation is fine.

  • Any hard constraints

    Data residency, regulatory obligations, procurement rules or a date you are working towards.

What happens next

How an engagement usually starts.

No obligation attaches to the first two steps.

  1. 01

    A reply from a person

    Either questions, or an early view on whether this is something we can help with.

  2. 02

    A short discovery call

    We talk through the problem, the constraints and what a good outcome would look like to you.

  3. 03

    A written proposal

    Scope, approach, what we would measure and what it would take. In plain language, not a slide deck.

  4. 04

    Discovery, then build

    If it makes sense for both sides, we start with the discovery phase described in our approach.

Before you write

Two things worth knowing up front.

Your information stays yours

We treat anything you send as confidential and will sign a mutual NDA before a detailed discussion. Please do not send personal data, credentials or production exports in a first email — we do not need them, and a description is enough to get started.

We will say when AI is the wrong tool

A number of the problems brought to us are better solved by fixing a data pipeline, a permission model or a process. If that is the case we will tell you, even when it means a smaller engagement.
