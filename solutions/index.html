The challenge

The AI Adoption Gap

Organizations are investing in AI. But having access to AI does not mean people are using it effectively.

01 / Awareness

Employees don't know the tools exist

New AI capabilities arrive faster than organizations can communicate them.

02 / Relevance

People can't see where AI fits their role

General awareness rarely becomes useful work in a team's daily tools.

03 / Value

Existing capabilities go underused

Microsoft 365 and Copilot may already be available without their full value being realized.

04 / Continuity

Adoption stops after training

A workshop starts awareness. Lasting use needs ongoing support and practical workflows.

05 / Direction

AI lacks a business roadmap

Individual experiments need a clear link to priorities, owners and measurable outcomes.

06 / Control

Security creates uncertainty

Teams need confidence in how data, access and agents are governed.
